### 1.1 Analytical derivation (performed before running any test, then verified empirically — not the reverse)

Before writing the test class, the exact mechanical outcome was hand-derived directly from the real, unmodified production code and catalog data, to have an independent check on the empirical test results (rather than writing tests and trusting them blind):

- `CatalogPhaseAllocationResolver.Resolve(candidate, targetWeekCount)` (`backend/RunningApp.Application/RuntimeCatalog/Schedule/Materialization/CatalogPhaseAllocation.cs`): anchors at each phase's `PreferredWeeks`, then compresses (if `targetWeekCount < sumPreferred`) or extends (if `>`) one week at a time in `CompressionPriority`/`ExtensionPriority` order, never past a phase's own `[MinimumWeeks, MaximumWeeks]` bound. `TEN_K_MASTER v11`'s real, frozen bounds: `FOUNDATION [2,3,4]`, `BUILD [3,4,5]`, `RACE_SPECIFIC [2,4,4]`, `TAPER [1,1,1]` (`plan-catalog/catalog/templates/ten-k-master.v11.json`), compression/extension priority `1,2,3,4` in that same phase order.
- Computing the resulting phase-week spans for every target 8-14: `8`→`F2,B3,RS2,T1` (RACE_SPECIFIC weeks 6-7), `9`→`F2,B3,RS3,T1` (weeks 6-8), `10`→`F2,B3,RS4,T1` (weeks 6-9), `11`→`F2,B4,RS4,T1` (weeks 7-10), `12`→`F3,B4,RS4,T1` (weeks 8-11, the catalog's own Preferred allocation), `13`→`F4,B4,RS4,T1` (weeks 9-12), `14`→`F4,B5,RS4,T1` (weeks 10-13).
- Applying `GEN.11 §1/§11`'s frozen global odd/even week-ordinal convention (week *N* is Pattern A/`KEY_SESSION`-carrying iff *N* is odd; `TAPER`'s own single week is force-overridden to Pattern A regardless of parity, per `GEN.11 §5`, and is therefore never at risk) to each RACE_SPECIFIC span: the count of real Pattern-A (lane-0-eligible) weeks within RACE_SPECIFIC's own span is **1** for both the 8-week span (weeks 6-7 → only week 7 is odd) and the 9-week span (weeks 6-8 → only week 7 is odd), and **2** for every span from 10 weeks up (10→{7,9}, 11→{7,9}, 12→{9,11}, 13→{9,11}, 14→{11,13}).
- RACE_SPECIFIC's combined halved minimum (`GEN.16`'s now-final Halving authority, §4 of the `TEN_K_WORKOUT_PROGRESSION_2D_V1 v1` table): `TEN_K_SPECIFIC_INTRO` (minimum 1, `COMPRESSIBLE`, so its own compression headroom is `1 - 1 = 0`) + `GOAL_PACE_REHEARSAL` (minimum 1, `PROTECTED`, never compressible) = **2**, with **zero** total compression headroom available anywhere in the phase (`ProgressionStageAllocator.ApplyCompression`'s own floor is `MinimumExposures - 1`, and both active stages are already at that floor or Protected).
- Therefore: at 8 and 9 weeks, `availableWeeks (1) < totalMinimum (2)`, deficit `1`, headroom `0` — `ApplyCompression` throws `ProgressionPhaseCapacityInsufficientException` (the guard doing its job, not a bug), exactly `GEN.17 §6`'s own diagnosed mechanism, just one week further than `GEN.17` itself tested. At 10 weeks and above, `availableWeeks (2) == totalMinimum (2)` exactly (or exceeds it, triggering `ApplyExtension` instead, e.g. `BUILD`'s own surplus at 14 weeks) — no capacity shortfall anywhere.
- `FOUNDATION`'s and `BUILD`'s own per-target Pattern-A availability was separately checked at every target 8-14 to confirm neither phase also fails: `FOUNDATION_EASY_BASE` (minimum 2, `COMPRESSIBLE`, headroom `2-1=1`) never has more than a 1-week deficit at any target's FOUNDATION span, and that deficit is always within its own 1-week headroom (compresses to 1, succeeds); `BUILD`'s two stages (`FARTLEK_INTRO` minimum 1 `COMPRESSIBLE` headroom 0, `THRESHOLD_INTRO` minimum 1 `COMPRESSIBLE` headroom 1) always meet or are extended above their combined minimum 2 at every target's BUILD span. Neither phase is ever the binding constraint at any of the 7 week counts — RACE_SPECIFIC is the sole, exclusive binding constraint throughout, for both levels, confirming `GEN.17`'s own finding generalizes rather than being joined by a second, independent shortfall.

This hand derivation was performed **before** writing or running `Gen18TwoDayCoreRepresentabilityBoundaryTests.cs`, specifically so the empirical test run would be a genuine confirmation (or falsification) of an independently-reasoned prediction, not a post-hoc rationalization of whatever the tests happened to show.
