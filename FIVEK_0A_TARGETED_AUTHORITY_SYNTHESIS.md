# FIVE-K.0A — Targeted FIVE_K Authority Synthesis (LIT-01 through LIT-05)

**Purpose:** Combine FIVEK-LIT-01 through FIVEK-LIT-05 into one answer to three questions — what can Appsel actually encode now, what remains a product choice, what remains unsupported. Does not repeat source summaries; see the five LIT files for full citation detail.

## 1. What can be encoded now (closed by synthesis or shared-authority confirmation)

- **Phase taxonomy**: Foundation/Build/RaceSpecific/Taper re-used as-is (LIT-04 Claim C — `CLOSED_SHARED_AUTHORITY`, confirmed, not merely assumed).
- **Workout-progression stage sequence** (not exposure counts): aerobic base → threshold/repetition development → VO2max/interval development → race-specific rehearsal → taper (LIT-04 Claim F, LIT-05 Claims A-C — `STRONG_MULTI_SOURCE_SYNTHESIS`).
- **Pace-role mapping for easy, threshold, and 5K race pace**: easy dominant volume category, threshold foundational/build-stage, 5K race pace race-specific and late-sequenced (LIT-05 Claims A, B, E) — all map to existing catalog `EASY`/`threshold-tempo` workouts with no new workout required.
- **Level differentiation in volume** (not exact numbers): Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced should differ materially, not share a flat default (LIT-01 Claim B — `DIRECT_CANONICAL_EVIDENCE` for the qualitative claim).
- **Long-run share mechanism with a generic ~20-30% band**: re-use the existing share-quadruple mechanism with values in the generic (not FIVE_K-unique) cross-distance band already shown to apply to 5K by at least one source (LIT-02 Claim A — `STRONG_MULTI_SOURCE_SYNTHESIS`).
- **Taper shape**: short (~1-2 weeks) with ~40-60% volume reduction and preserved intensity/frequency (LIT-03 Claims A-C — `STRONG_MULTI_SOURCE_SYNTHESIS`).
- **Growth-rate cap and calibration-volume mechanisms**: confirmed applicable with no FIVE_K-specific override found (LIT-01 Claims E-F — `CLOSED_SHARED_AUTHORITY_CONFIRMED`).
- **Fitness-evidence hierarchy and Riegel projection mechanism**: reconfirmed with no FIVE_K-specific contradiction (LIT-05 Claim G).

## 2. What remains a product choice (evidence-bounded, not evidence-determined)

1. **CoreCycle min/preferred/max week triple** — evidence supports an 8-13 week structured-plan range; no source states a deterministic triple (LIT-04 Claim B).
2. **Exact per-phase week counts** — evidence supports proportion only (~60-70% Foundation+Build, ~30-40% RaceSpecific+Taper of a 12-week-class plan) (LIT-04 Claim D).
3. **Compression/extension priority order** — a principle (Foundation most elastic) is cross-source-consistent but not a directly stated rule (LIT-04 Claim E).
4. **Exact peak-volume value per Level×Frequency cell** — only Intermediate×4D/5D has even an approximate cross-checked band; all other cells lack one (LIT-01 Claims A, D).
5. **Whether an absolute long-run ceiling is added (particularly Advanced, informed by Higdon's single-source 19.3km data point) or share-only governance is kept** (LIT-02 Claims B-C).
6. **Exact taper week count (1 vs. 2) and exact multiplier(s) within the ~0.40-0.60 reduction band** (LIT-03 Claims D-E).
7. **Whether V1 includes a dedicated VO2max-interval/faster-than-5K workout tier**, evidenced as a legitimate option but not required for a coherent minimum plan (LIT-05 Claim D — `OPTIONAL_SUPPORTED`, not `CANONICAL_REQUIRED`).

These seven form the smallest product-decision set; none was approved in this research phase (governing-prompt §53 — research phases do not self-approve product defaults).

## 3. What remains genuinely unsupported (source silence / conflicting, not a product choice)

- **Exact min/preferred/max workout-progression exposure counts per stage** — sample-schedule occurrence counts exist, but no source states them as rules (LIT-04 Claim G) — `SOURCE_SILENCE_REMAINS`.
- **Independent Level×Frequency matrix** — every source conflates level and frequency (one frequency per level), so no source independently supports any cell outside the three level-frequency pairings the sources happen to publish together (LIT-01 Claim C, LIT-02 Claim D) — `SOURCE_SILENCE_REMAINS`.
- **Exact quantitative intensity-distribution ratio** (e.g., a specific 80/20-style split) — the field itself is actively contested (one secondary source explicitly rebuts the 80/20 consensus claim) — `CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE`, not resolved here (LIT-05 Claim F).
- **Exact FIVE_K taper activation-workout content** — qualitative principle (retain intensity) is synthesized, exact content is not (LIT-03 Claim C) — `SOURCE_SILENCE_REMAINS`.

## 4. Net effect on FIVE-K.0's 14/15-row closure matrix

See the phase report's required closure matrix (§39) for the row-by-row mapping. In aggregate: of the rows this phase's five LIT items targeted, none closes to `CLOSED_DIRECT_EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE` outright (no row has a single, directly-stated, deterministic numeric rule from one authoritative primary source). Several close to `CLOSED_MULTI_SOURCE_SYNTHESIS` for structural/qualitative content (phase sequence, workout-progression stage order, pace-role mapping, long-run-share mechanism applicability, taper shape). Several remain `EVIDENCE_INFORMED_PRODUCT_DEFAULT_REQUIRED` for the exact numeric values runtime ultimately needs. A smaller remainder stays genuinely `SOURCE_SILENCE_REMAINS` (exposure counts, independent Level×Frequency matrix, taper activation content) or becomes the one `CONFLICTING_EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE` row (intensity-distribution ratio).

## 5. Minimum implementable V1 implication (preview — full treatment in phase report §52)

The evidence most completely covers **Intermediate level, 4D (and loosely 5D) frequency**: this is the one cell where volume (LIT-01 Claim D), long-run share (LIT-02), taper shape (LIT-03), and workout-progression/pace roles (LIT-04/05) all have at least `EVIDENCE_INFORMED_PRODUCT_DEFAULT_REQUIRED`-grade coverage simultaneously. Beginner and Advanced cells, and any frequency outside 4D/5D, have materially thinner coverage and are the natural candidates to defer past V1.
