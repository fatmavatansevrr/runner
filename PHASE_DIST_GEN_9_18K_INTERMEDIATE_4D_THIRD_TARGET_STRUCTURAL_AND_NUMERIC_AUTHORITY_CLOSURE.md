## 27. Supplemental 18K evidence

Narrow web research was performed, targeting approximately-18K/near-HM race preparation for intermediate recreational runners at roughly 4 days/week. **Finding: genuine sparsity.** Multiple targeted queries ("18K training plan," "11 mile race training plan," "18km race training plan," "20K race training plan") consistently returned marathon and half-marathon training-plan content misattributed via "18-week"/"20-week" plan-duration keyword overlap, not genuine 18-kilometer or 20-kilometer race-distance content — confirming 18K (and, incidentally, 20K) are not standardized, commercially-supported race distances with dedicated English-language training-plan literature, unlike 10-mile and 15K.
